# PGH Pediatrics Exam Studio

---

Open [index.html](index.html). Choose the Nelson bank (6,700 questions) or [Boards Mock 2025](boards-mock.html) (1,000 questions).

All 1,000 mock questions have Easy, Average, or Difficult labels. These are rule-assisted editorial estimates with manual calibration, not empirical difficulty measurements. The 53 formerly held questions were rewritten or corrected against cited sources, with separate source and key-hidden presentation review by Codex. No held questions remain. The remaining 947 questions keep their earlier content and have not received a new clinical review.

The exam maker supports single-level, mixed, and custom difficulty draws. The mock browser has difficulty and repaired-item filters, explanations, references, and preserved original provenance. Revised question content invalidates incompatible saved answers; unchanged answers can be restored by progress import. Changing this folder may require exporting progress from the older copy and importing it here.

The original Nelson bank and its self-study pages are unchanged. Each institution retains its own branding and progress namespace. Everything works offline. Give examinees only the generated question PDF; the studio HTML contains answers for examiner and self-study use.
